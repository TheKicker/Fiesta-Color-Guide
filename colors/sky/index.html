Sky

In production

2024-present · Color No. 349

Introducing Sky, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2024 to current production. It's a serene and calming shade of blue, reminiscent of the vast expanse of the sky on a clear day. Sky adds a touch of tranquility and elegance to any tablescape. Mix and match Sky with colors like Ivory and Meadow for a peaceful and sophisticated table setting, or pair it with Twilight and Butterscotch for a more vibrant and dynamic combination.

Notes on Sky

Sky arrived in 2024 and is still in production. It's a pale, soft blue with very little saturation - a quiet color, closer in behavior to a neutral than to Turquoise or Lapis. The swing from Twilight, the near-navy it overlapped, is the largest jump the blue family has ever made: about ΔE 71. It arrived in a big year of change, with Lemongrass, Mulberry and Butterscotch all retiring at once. Because it carries so little color, it will sit next to almost anything, which makes it one of the easier current colors to add to an existing collection.

Using Sky on a table

Sky is soft enough to sit beside almost anything. It works as a bridge between two stronger colors that would otherwise argue. At L* 84 it is one of the paler glazes in the line: dark food reads sharply against it, pale food much less so. It nearly disappears against a white cloth (1.5:1), so it shows best on dark wood or a colored runner.

Where Sky sits in the line

Sky arrived while Twilight was still in production, and is lighter and more muted than it - a complete change of direction rather than a reformulation (ΔE 71.5). It lasted 2 years where the median for its era is 7, which is why it turns up so rarely. In 2024 it took the place of Ivory, Lemongrass, Mulberry and Butterscotch on the shelf.
