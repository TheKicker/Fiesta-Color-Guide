Lavender

In production

2026–present · Color No. 351

Meet Lavender, Fiesta’s new color for 2026, and 90th anniversary color, continuing into current production. Inspired by the soft and delicate beauty, this gentle tone adds a sense of calm to everyday dining and creates a kitchen atmosphere that feels fresh, welcoming, and effortlessly timeless. Pair Lavender with colors like Linen or Sky for a soft, harmonious look - for a more striking contrast try Plum or Mulberry.
