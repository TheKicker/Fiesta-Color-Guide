Pearl Gray

Retired

1999-2001 · Color No. 118

Introducing Pearl Gray, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 1999 to 2001. It's a subtle and sophisticated shade of gray with hints of pearl-like shimmer, reminiscent of soft moonlight on a cloudy night. Pearl Gray adds a touch of elegance and refinement to any tablescape. Mix and match Pearl Gray with colors like White and Turquoise for a classic and timeless table setting, or pair it with Plum and Slate for a contemporary and chic combination.

Notes on Pearl Gray

Pearl Gray ran two years, 1999 to 2001, and it's the most genuinely neutral color Fiesta has ever made - almost no color in it at all, just a cool, soft gray. That short run makes it scarce, and its quietness makes it easy to overlook in a listing photo, where it can pass for White in poor light. It replaced Chartreuse on the shelf when it arrived. If you like the idea of it, vintage Gray is the closest relative, and the two sit only a few ΔE apart - near enough to mix, far enough to notice.

Using Pearl Gray on a table

Pearl Gray carries almost no color of its own, so it is the layer everything else sits on. Build the table out of it and add one loud piece. At L* 82 it is one of the paler glazes in the line: dark food reads sharply against it, pale food much less so. It nearly disappears against a white cloth (1.6:1), so it shows best on dark wood or a colored runner.

Where Pearl Gray sits in the line

Pearl Gray arrived while White was still in production, and is noticeably darker than it - a clearly different glaze rather than a reformulation (ΔE 11.2). It lasted 2 years where the median for its era is 7, which is why it turns up so rarely. In 1999 it took the place of Chartreuse, 1997-1999 on the shelf. What else happened in 1999 →
