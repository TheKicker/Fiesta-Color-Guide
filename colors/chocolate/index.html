Chocolate

Retired

2008–2011 · Color No. 331

Introducing Chocolate, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2008 to 2011. It's a rich and decadent shade of brown, reminiscent of velvety smooth chocolate. Chocolate adds a warm and inviting touch to any tablescape. Mix and match Chocolate with colors like Ivory and Sunflower for a cozy and rustic table setting, or pair it with Plum and Slate for a more refined and sophisticated combination.

If you own Chocolate, set the table with these

5 combinations, every one built only from colors Fiesta has actually produced. Each says which rule it follows, and which pieces you can still buy today.
