Mulberry

Retired

2018–2024 · Color No. 343

Introducing Mulberry, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2018 to 2024. It's a rich and luxurious shade of purple, reminiscent of the deep hue of ripe mulberries. Mulberry adds a touch of elegance and sophistication to any tablescape. Mix and match Mulberry with colors like Ivory and Lemongrass for a refined and romantic table setting, or pair it with Plum and Poppy for a more dramatic and festive combination.

Notes on Mulberry

Mulberry ran six years, 2018 to 2024, and it's a deep, dark purple with a lot of red in it. It arrived the year Fiesta opened its Atlanta showroom, replacing Sage on the shelf. The thing to know: it sits only ΔE 5 from Plum, which retired in 2015 — close enough that side by side most people can't reliably separate them, and close enough that they shouldn't really share a table. It retired in 2024 with Lemongrass and Butterscotch. Lavender, the 2026 color, is much lighter and represents a complete change of direction for the purple family.

Using Mulberry on a table

Mulberry has enough color to be noticed without taking over, which makes it a good second or third piece — a salad plate or a serving bowl rather than the whole setting. At L* 15 it is a genuinely dark plate, so pale food stands out on it and dark food disappears. It separates strongly from a white cloth (15.1:1) and less so from dark wood.
