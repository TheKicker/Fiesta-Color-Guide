Heather

2006-2008 · Color No. 328

Introducing Heather, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2006 to 2008. It's a soft and subtle shade of purple, reminiscent of blooming heather flowers in a tranquil meadow. Heather adds a touch of tranquility and elegance to any tablescape. Mix and match Heather with colors like White and Sunflower for a delicate and romantic table setting, or pair it with Plum and Slate for a more sophisticated and serene combination.

Notes on Heather

Heather managed two years, 2006 to 2008, which puts it among the scarcer Post 86 colors. It's a muted, grayish purple - much softer and lighter than the Plum it overlapped with, and genuinely hard to photograph accurately, which is why listings for it vary so wildly. It replaced Periwinkle Blue on the shelf. Claret, which arrived eight years later, is the closest Fiesta has come to it since. If you like quiet, complicated colors rather than bright ones, this is one of the more interesting things the company has made, and it doesn't come up often.

Using Heather on a table

Heather is soft enough to sit beside almost anything. It works as a bridge between two stronger colors that would otherwise argue. At L* 29 it is a genuinely dark plate, so pale food stands out on it and dark food disappears. It separates strongly from a white cloth (9.7:1) and less so from dark wood.

Where Heather sits in the line

Heather arrived while Plum was still in production, and is lighter and more muted than it - a clearly different glaze rather than a reformulation (ΔE 15.8). It lasted 2 years where the median for its era is 7, which is why it turns up so rarely. In 2006 it took the place of Periwinkle Blue on the shelf. What else happened in 2006 →
