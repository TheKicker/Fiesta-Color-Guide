Jade

In production

2023–present · Color No. 348

Introducing Jade, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2023 to current production. It's a rich and vibrant shade of green, reminiscent of the luscious hue of jade gemstones. Jade adds a touch of natural beauty and sophistication to any tablescape. Mix and match Jade with colors like Ivory and Meadow for a fresh and elegant table setting, or pair it with Twilight and Butterscotch for a more dynamic and luxurious combination.

Notes on Jade

Jade arrived in 2023 and is still in production. It's a deep, cool green — considerably darker than the Meadow it overlapped with, and one of the few genuinely dark colors currently available. That depth makes it behave more like a neutral anchor than an accent: pale food reads beautifully on it. It's the closest thing in current production to the original 1936 Light Green (ΔE 10), which makes it a reasonable modern partner for vintage pieces. With Evergreen long retired, Jade is the only dark green Fiesta currently makes.

Using Jade on a table

Jade has enough color to be noticed without taking over, which makes it a good second or third piece — a salad plate or a serving bowl rather than the whole setting. At L* 27 it is a genuinely dark plate, so pale food stands out on it and dark food disappears. It separates strongly from a white cloth (10.6:1) and less so from dark wood.

Where Jade sits in the line

Jade arrived while Meadow was still in production, and is noticeably darker than it — a complete change of direction rather than a reformulation (ΔE 27.5). It lasted 3 years where the median for its era is 7, which is why it turns up so rarely. What else happened at the company in 2023 →
