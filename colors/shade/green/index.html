Shade family

Fiesta green colors

Green is Fiesta's largest family - thirteen colors, more than any other - running from the original Light Green in 1936 to Jade in 2023. That is not an accident. Green sits comfortably beside warm and cool colors alike in a way no other hue quite manages, and its associations with growth and the outdoors make it an easy color to put food on. Fiesta's greens have swung from the acid Chartreuse and Lemongrass to the near-black Evergreen and Jade. Medium Green, retired in 1969, is widely regarded among collectors as the hardest vintage color to find.

Fiesta has used 13 green glazes since 1936, starting with Green (Light) and most recently Jade in 2023. 2 are in production today: Meadow, Jade. They run from Sea Mist Green at the light end to Evergreen at the dark end; Shamrock has had the longest run of any green.
