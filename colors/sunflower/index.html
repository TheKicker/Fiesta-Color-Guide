Sunflower

Retired

2001-2026 · Color No. 320

Introducing Sunflower, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2001 to 2026. It's a cheerful and vibrant shade of yellow, reminiscent of bright sunflowers swaying in the summer breeze. Sunflower adds a burst of energy and warmth to any tablescape. Mix and match Sunflower with colors like Cobalt and Shamrock for a lively and dynamic table setting, or pair it with Plum and Slate for a more subdued yet sophisticated combination.

Notes on Sunflower

Sunflower had a twenty-five-year run, 2001 to 2026, making it one of the longest-lived colors in the modern line - and it only just retired, alongside Twilight, when Lavender arrived for the ninetieth anniversary. It's a warm, bright yellow with real saturation. Because it was made for so long, it's easy to find second-hand and will stay that way, which makes it one of the safest retired colors to build around. It's also close enough to the Post 86 Yellow it replaced (ΔE 4) that the two mix without any visible seam.

Using Sunflower on a table

Sunflower carries enough color to lead a table on its own. Use it for the plates and let quieter pieces do the rest; two or three settings of it go a long way. At L* 88 it is one of the paler glazes in the line: dark food reads sharply against it, pale food much less so. It nearly disappears against a white cloth (1.4:1), so it shows best on dark wood or a colored runner.

Where Sunflower sits in the line

Sunflower arrived while Yellow, 1987-2002 was still in production, and is noticeably more vivid than it - a close relative rather than a reformulation (ΔE 4.4). It has been in production 25 years against a median of 7 for its era - one of the colors the company kept. In 2001 it arrived alongside Plum and took the place of Pearl Gray and Juniper on the shelf.
