Butterscotch

Retired

2020–2024 · Color No. 345

Introducing Butterscotch, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2020 to 2024. It's a warm and inviting shade of orange, reminiscent of the sweet and creamy flavor of butterscotch candy. Butterscotch adds a cozy and nostalgic touch to any tablescape. Mix and match Butterscotch with colors like Ivory and Meadow for a cheerful and autumnal table setting, or pair it with Plum and Poppy for a more vibrant and festive combination.
