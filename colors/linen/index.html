Linen

In production

2025–present · Color No. 350

Introducing Linen, Fiesta's new color for 2025, continuing into current production. This light cream hue offers a quiet sophistication, echoing the charm of Ivory with a modern twist. Linen is ideal for a serene tablescape; pair it with colors like Sage for calm, or Scarlet for a bold contrast, perfect for all dining occasions.

Notes on Linen

Linen arrived in 2025 and is still in production, taking over as the warm neutral after Ivory retired the year before. It's a light cream, slightly cooler and more muted than Ivory was. The thing worth knowing: it sits only ΔE 3 from Old Ivory, one of the original 1936 colors — which makes Linen the closest thing you can currently buy to the neutral Fiesta launched with ninety years ago. If you're building a vintage-look table with modern pieces, that's a genuinely useful fact. As a ground color it takes any accent you put on it.

Using Linen on a table

Linen carries almost no color of its own, so it is the layer everything else sits on. Build the table out of it and add one loud piece. At L* 91 it is one of the paler glazes in the line: dark food reads sharply against it, pale food much less so. It nearly disappears against a white cloth (1.3:1), so it shows best on dark wood or a colored runner.

Where Linen sits in the line

Linen took over from Ivory as Fiesta's white, and is noticeably more muted than it — a close relative rather than a reformulation (ΔE 6.6). It lasted 1 year where the median for its era is 7, which is why it turns up so rarely. What else happened at the company in 2025 →
