Marigold

Retired

2008–2012 · Color No. 333

Introducing Marigold, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 2008 to 2012. It's a vibrant and sunny shade of yellow, reminiscent of the cheerful blooms of marigold flowers. Marigold adds a burst of energy and warmth to any tablescape. Mix and match Marigold with colors like Ivory and Lemongrass for a bright and summery table setting, or pair it with Plum and Slate for a more sophisticated and autumnal combination.

Notes on Marigold

Marigold started life as a limited-edition color — it was introduced in 2008 on a 3-piece baking bowl set for the company's 75th anniversary, one of three colors that year, and later became the anniversary color for the full 2011 celebration. It ran to 2012. It's a deep, saturated golden orange-yellow, noticeably darker and richer than the Sunflower it overlapped with. That anniversary connection makes it a color collectors seek out for reasons beyond the shade itself. Daffodil, which arrived in 2017, sits fairly close and is still in production.

Using Marigold on a table

Marigold carries enough color to lead a table on its own. Use it for the plates and let quieter pieces do the rest; two or three settings of it go a long way. Its mid lightness (L* 76) means most food reads against it without either washing out or vanishing. It nearly disappears against a white cloth (1.9:1), so it shows best on dark wood or a colored runner.

Where Marigold sits in the line

Marigold arrived while Sunflower was still in production, and is darker and more vivid than it — a clearly different glaze rather than a reformulation (ΔE 15.1). In 2008 it arrived alongside Chocolate and Ivory and took the place of Persimmon and Heather on the shelf. What else happened in 2008 →
