1993-1995 · Color No. 113

Introducing Lilac, made by The Fiesta Tableware Company from 1993 to 1995. It's a delicate and enchanting shade of purple, reminiscent of blooming lilac flowers in springtime. Lilac adds a touch of elegance and romance to any tablescape. Mix and match Lilac with colors like White and Sunflower for a soft and feminine table setting, or pair it with Shamrock and Tangerine for a vibrant and playful combination.

Notes on Lilac

Lilac is the one to know about. It was Fiesta's first purple, introduced in 1993 - nearly sixty years into the line - and it lasted only two years before being retired in 1995. That short run makes it one of the hardest Post 86 colors to assemble a full set in, and prices reflect it. It's a mid-depth purple with real blue in it, more muted than you might expect from the name. Lavender, the 2026 anniversary color, is the closest thing Fiesta has made since, and it's noticeably lighter. If you find Lilac in the wild at a reasonable price, that's a good day.

Using Lilac on a table

Lilac has enough color to be noticed without taking over, which makes it a good second or third piece - a salad plate or a serving bowl rather than the whole setting. Its mid lightness (L* 48) means most food reads against it without either washing out or vanishing. It separates strongly from a white cloth (4.8:1) and less so from dark wood.
