Medium Green

Retired

1959–1969

Crafted between 1959 and 1969 - this iconic color choice opens the door to creative table arrangements, as its balanced green hue adds a refreshing touch to your dining setting. Combine Medium Green with complementary colors like crisp white or sunny yellow for a lively and cheerful ambiance, or create a nature-inspired theme by pairing it with earthy browns and subtle creams.
