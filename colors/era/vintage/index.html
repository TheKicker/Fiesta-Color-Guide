Vintage Fiesta colors, 1936-1972

The colors of the original Fiesta line, from its debut at the Pittsburgh China & Glass Show in January 1936 to the retirement of the line in 1973. These pieces predate Fiesta's color numbering entirely, so a vintage color is identified by its name, its shape and its glaze - never by a number on the back.
