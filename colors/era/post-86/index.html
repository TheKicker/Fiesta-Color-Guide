Post 86 Fiesta colors, 1986 to today

Fiesta returned in March 1986 for its 50th anniversary, in a vitrified, lead-free body with an updated palette. Collectors call everything from that point on "Post 86." Each of these carries a color number, which is the fastest way to identify a modern piece.
